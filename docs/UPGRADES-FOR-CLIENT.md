# Base Camp Adventure: website upgrades

A simple list of what is already done, what we are building now, and what we
can add next. Each idea has a short reason so it's easy to decide.

---

## Already done

- **New fast website** with the same page addresses as before, so Google rankings are kept.
- **Our own admin panel** (admin.basecampadventure.com) to edit trips, blogs, pages, photos, reviews, team and the homepage.
- **Online booking and payment** with WeTravel, plus discount codes.
- **Professional emails** to travellers and the office, a newsletter design, and gift vouchers.
- **Strong security**: two-step sign-in, protection against hackers and password guessing.
- **SEO fixed**: better page titles and descriptions, and every technical check passes.

---

## Just finished (switch each one on or off in Admin → Settings → Features)

| Feature | What it does | Why it helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On/off switches** | Turn any feature on or off from the admin. | Full control without a developer. |
| **Reminder for unpaid bookings** | If someone starts a booking but doesn't pay, they get a friendly reminder after 24 hours. | Brings back customers who forgot or got distracted. |
| **WhatsApp alerts** | The office gets a WhatsApp message for every new booking, enquiry and payment. | Faster replies; you never miss a customer. |
| **tawk.to chat details** | When someone books or asks a question, their name, email and booking number show in the chat. | Your team knows who they are talking to. |
| **Reviews after the trip** | After the trip, travellers get an email to rate it with stars and write a review. You approve good ones and they appear on the trip page. | More real reviews mean more trust and better Google results. |
| **Traveller account (my.basecampadventure.com)** | After payment, and only when you approve it, the traveller gets a private account to see their trip and payments, upload their passport and insurance, add flight details and download their documents. The account closes itself after the trip. | No more chasing documents by email; everything for each trip is in one safe place. |
| **German, French and Spanish pages** | Trips in three more languages, each switchable on or off. Everest Base Camp is already translated; the other top trips are translated in the admin (Trips → the trip → Translations). | These are big trekking markets, and people book more in their own language. |

## Ideas we can add next

| # | Feature | What it does | Why it helps | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Fixed departure dates** | Show group dates on each trip with seats left (for example "12 Oct, only 3 seats left"). | "Only a few seats left" is the strongest reason people book now instead of later. | Medium |
| 2 | **Prices in the visitor's money** | Show prices in euros, pounds or Australian dollars next to US dollars. | Travellers understand the price straight away. | Small |
| 3 | **Trip finder quiz** | A few easy questions (days, fitness, budget, month) that suggest the best 3 trips. | Helps first-time visitors who don't know Nepal. | Small |
| 4 | **Compare trips** | See two or three treks side by side: days, height, difficulty, price. | Helps people who can't decide. | Small |
| 5 | **Emails before the trip** | Automatic emails 30 days and 7 days before departure (packing list, visa, airport pickup). | Travellers arrive ready, and the office saves time. | Small |
| 6 | **Online forms for safety** | Travellers sign the waiver and fill in the medical form online before they arrive. | No paper forms in Kathmandu. | Small |
| 7 | **Sales dashboard** | Charts for bookings, money received, best-selling trips and where customers come from. | See how the business is doing at a glance. | Medium |
| 8 | **Departure list (PDF)** | For each departure: who is going, rooms, food needs, guide and porters. | The field team gets everything on one sheet. | Medium |
| 9 | **Guide planner** | Choose which guide leads which departure and see who is free. | No double bookings of guides. | Medium |
| 10 | **Quote maker** | Make a tailor-made price offer as a nice PDF with a "Pay deposit" button. | Faster, more professional replies to custom requests. | Medium |
| 11 | **Month pages** | Pages like "Everest Base Camp in October" made from our trips and guides. | People search like this; these pages bring visitors from Google. | Small |
| 12 | **Save a trip** | Visitors save trips they like and get a reminder email later. | Brings visitors back to book. | Small |
| 13 | **Trip of the month** | A banner on the website to promote one trip, set from the admin. | Easy way to push a trip that needs bookings. | Small |
| 14 | **Weather and best months** | A simple chart on each trip showing the best months to go. | Answers the most common question before people ask. | Small |
| 15 | **Trip brochure (PDF)** | A good-looking PDF of each trip with photos and the altitude chart. | Easy to share with friends and family who travel together. | Small |
| 16 | **More languages** | Chinese, Japanese, Hindi and others, after German, French and Spanish. | Reach new countries once the first three work well. | Medium |

**Size:** Small = a few days. Medium = about a week or more.

---

## Our suggestion for what to do first

1. **Fixed departure dates**: brings in the most bookings.
2. **Emails before the trip** and the **online safety forms**: save the office a lot of time.
3. **Sales dashboard**: shows clearly what is working.
